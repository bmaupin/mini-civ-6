# CivRev game mode

## Design philosophy

This game mode attempts to reproduce the feel of CivRev as much as reasonably possible within Civ 6. This is not a one-for-one reproduction of CivRev.

## Motivation

CivRev is a unique take on Civilization from the creator of Civilization himself (Sid Meier). It offers a streamlined experience that is appealing for many who do not have the time or patience to deal with the increasing complexity of modern Civilization games. However, it was never released on PC and it's getting more and more difficult to play on other devices as it's been delisted from multiple stores.

## Features

### Armadas and armies available from the start

Armadas and armies (and corps and fleets) are available from the start of the game.

### Automated tile improvements

Tile improvements are fully automated and builders have been removed from the game.

### Civ 6 game systems not in CivRev removed

Amenities, city states, districts\*, policy cards, religion have been removed from the game.

\*Spaceport has been kept as it's needed for a science victory

### Less of everything

Buildings, civics, diplomatic actions, governments, improvements, resources, techs, units, wonders have been greatly reduced to better match those of CivRev.

### UI

Game starts zoomed in to better match CivRev zoom and allows for a much closer zoom in level.
