# Amenities

## Problems

- One more system to worry about
- It's a bit of an analogue to the Civ 5 happiness system, not exactly since that system was to prevent infinite city sprawl, but nonetheless it feels superfluous
- When a city doesn't have enough amenities, yields and growth are decreased, and it can ultimately lead to revolt
- Ultimately it's just one more system in the game which adds more decision points and may or may not be interesting

## Solutions

Thankfully it's pretty easy to remove this from the game altogether by disabling the amenities system and removing all database items (buildings, districts, etc) related to it.

It still shows up in the City Details dialogue but this may actually be desirable as it gives a visual indicator that amenities are disabled for the game.
